# 01 — BTL & OFFLINE MARKETING

## Slide 4 — BTL & Offline Marketing

### Your Responsibility
Assigned locations mein various BTL & Offline Marketing Activities ko plan aur execute karna.

### Activities may include
- Leaflet Distribution
- Kiosk Activities
- Hoardings
- Branding
- Local Promotions
- Society Activities
- Events
- Other relevant campaigns

### Core Principle
Your job is **NOT only to execute**.

**Plan → Execute → Monitor → Measure → Report**

## Slide 5 — How to Execute a BTL Activity?

### BEFORE ACTIVITY
- Approved plan samjho
- Location confirm karo
- Target audience identify karo
- Vendor/agency coordinate karo
- Manpower arrange karo
- Marketing material check karo
- Branding assets check karo

### DURING ACTIVITY
- Brand guidelines follow karo
- Activity quality monitor karo
- Customer engagement ensure karo
- Lead opportunities identify karo
- Activity photographs/proof collect karo

### AFTER ACTIVITY
- Lead data update karo
- Activity details report karo
- Issues/feedback share karo
- Lead generation analyse karo

## Slide 6 — Activity Quality Checklist

Activity execute karte waqt check karein:

- Correct location
- Correct branding
- Proper manpower
- Marketing material available
- Brand guidelines followed
- Customer engagement
- Lead collection
- Activity photographs
- Timely reporting

### Remember
> Activity complete hona enough nahi hai. Activity properly execute hona important hai.

# WEB EXPERIENCE REQUIREMENTS

## Core Interaction
The content should behave like a presentation, but technically be a responsive website.

### Navigation
- Mouse wheel / trackpad scroll moves one presentation section at a time.
- Arrow Down / Page Down moves to the next section.
- Arrow Up / Page Up moves to the previous section.
- Home moves to the first section.
- End moves to the final section.
- Space can advance to the next section.
- Avoid uncontrolled long-page scrolling; use presentation-style section snapping.
- Touch/swipe should work on mobile/tablet.

### Presentation Mode
- Full-screen presentation mode.
- Visible Fullscreen control.
- Optional keyboard shortcut such as `F`.
- Progress indicator showing current slide / total slides.
- Optional section title indicator.

### UX
- Every slide should feel like a designed presentation screen, not a document page.
- Use strong visual hierarchy.
- Keep copy concise on-screen while preserving the source meaning.
- Use progressive reveal/animation where useful.
- Avoid excessive animation that slows training.
- Maintain consistent spacing, typography, icons, cards, diagrams and transitions.
- Responsive across desktop, tablet and mobile.
- Keyboard accessible.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Content Integrity
- Do not invent additional claims, KPIs, policies, processes or business facts.
- Preserve the original Hindi-English/Hinglish language and terminology.
- Do not silently change the meaning of the source material.
- Visual metaphors and UI labels may be added only when they clarify existing content.
